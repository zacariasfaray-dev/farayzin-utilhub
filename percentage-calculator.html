Percentages, three ways

Discounts, price increases, test scores, tips — pick the question you need answered and get the result as you type.

How to use it

"What is X% of Y" answers discount-style questions: 15% of $200 = $30. Type the percentage in X and the total in Y.

"X is what percent of Y" is for scores and shares: you scored 45 points out of 60 — that's 75%.

"Percentage change" compares two values: rent went from $800 to $920, which is a 15% increase. If the result is negative, the value went down.

Percentage FAQ

Why does percentage change use the old value as the base?

Because change is always relative to where you started. Going from 800 to 920 is +120 on a base of 800 (15%), not on 920 (13%) — using the starting value keeps increases and decreases symmetric.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes. A negative in the change mode means the value dropped; a negative percentage in "what is X% of Y" gives a negative result, which is usually not what you want — double-check the sign.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded, the calculation happens entirely on your device.
