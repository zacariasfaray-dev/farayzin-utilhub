What's a normal tip?

United States: 15–20% at restaurants is standard, 20%+ for excellent service; bars typically a dollar or two per drink. Taxi and delivery usually 10–15%.

Europe and much of the world: service is often included — rounding up or 5–10% is a friendly gesture rather than an obligation. Check the bill first: "serviço" or "service compris" means it's already there.

Splitting fairly: the per-person figure here divides bill + tip evenly. If one person ordered much more, it's polite to adjust up from this baseline rather than down.

Tip FAQ

Should I tip on tax?

Custom in the US is to tip on the pre-tax total, though many people simply tip on the final number for convenience — a slightly generous habit, never an offensive one.

Does it work with any currency?

Yes — the calculator is currency-agnostic. Type the amount in your local money and read the result in the same unit.

Can I use decimal tip rates?

Yes, the slider moves in 1% steps, but this page accepts any custom rate by editing the number — precision welcome.
