How randomness works here

Fair means uniform. This tool uses crypto.getRandomValues — the same cryptographic source browsers use for security — so no number in your range is favored. Lottery-style draws and giveaway raffles can trust the pick.

Unique mode is for draws without replacement: picking 3 winners from 500 entries gives you 3 different numbers, like drawing names from a hat without putting them back.

Random FAQ

Can I pick 6 lottery numbers from 1–60?

Yes — set minimum 1, maximum 60, quantity 6, and "unique picks" on. Note that official lottery entries still need a real ticket; this is for games, simulations and classroom stats.

Is it better than Math.random()?

Math.random is fine for casual use but its quality isn't guaranteed. The cryptographic source used here has no known bias, which matters when the pick must be defensible.

Why did my unique request do nothing?

Unique mode needs enough room: you can't draw 10 unique numbers from a range of 5. Widen the range or reduce the quantity.
