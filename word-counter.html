When writers use this

Essays and applications usually have hard limits — count characters with spaces before submitting, because most systems count them that way.

Social posts and ads live and die by length: keep headlines under 60 characters and meta descriptions under 160 so search engines don't cut them off.

Articles and blogs are judged partly by depth — a 1,000-word piece reads in about 4 minutes at an average pace of 250 words per minute, which is the estimate shown here.

Counter FAQ

How is reading time calculated?

Words ÷ 250 words per minute, rounded up to the nearest half minute. It's an average — slow, careful reading takes longer.

What counts as a sentence?

Text followed by a period, question mark or exclamation mark. Consecutive punctuation (like "…") counts once.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The counting runs in your browser as you type; nothing leaves your device.
