The units, in plain terms

Length covers kilometers, meters, centimeters, miles, feet and inches — travel distances, room sizes and heights all use these.

Weight covers kilograms, grams, pounds and ounces — recipes and luggage limits mostly. 1 kg equals 2.20462 lbs exactly.

Temperature converts Celsius and Fahrenheit both ways. Remember the scales cross at −40°: minus 40°C is minus 40°F, the one number that's the same everywhere.

Converter FAQ

How accurate are the results?

Factors are exact by definition where possible (1 inch = 25.4 mm, 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg) and results show up to 6 significant digits — far beyond daily-life needs.

Why is −40 the same in Celsius and Fahrenheit?

Because the two scales use different zero points and step sizes; −40 is where their lines cross. Below that, Fahrenheit reads colder; above, Celsius eventually catches up at the crossing point only.

Does it need internet?

No — after the page loads, all conversion math runs locally on your device.
